# Calibration gate

`full` is a release suite, not an author estimate. Each task needs at least one successful independent professional baseline, a median active time of at least 120 minutes, and a two-reviewer quality record.

Copy the templates into `tasks/<task-id>/baselines/`. Replace every placeholder with real evidence. Do not commit names, email addresses, raw recordings, credentials, or private work product. `participantId` is a one-way SHA-256 pseudonym; `evidenceDigest` pins separately retained evidence.

The CLI rejects `--suite full` until every record validates and matches the task and benchmark versions.
